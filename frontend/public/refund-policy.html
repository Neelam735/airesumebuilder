Refund & Cancellation Policy

App: AI Resume Builder
Effective date: 2 September 2026
Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains when a payment made in AI Resume Builder can be refunded and how to request one. It applies to the one-time ₹29 charge for downloading an AI-enhanced resume.

1. What you are paying for

AI Resume Builder sells a digital product delivered immediately: the download of your AI-enhanced resume as a PDF or Word document. There is nothing physical to ship, and no subscription — it is a single, one-time charge of ₹29 (inclusive of applicable taxes).

Building, editing, previewing, and the AI enhancement itself are free. You can see the enhanced result before paying. Payment is required only at the moment you choose to download it — so you know exactly what you are buying before you are charged.

2. Cancellation

  • There is no subscription and no recurring billing, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing will ever renew automatically.
  • You may cancel a payment at any point before completing it, by closing the Google Play or Razorpay checkout screen. You will not be charged.
  • You can stop using the Service at any time by uninstalling the app.

3. When we will refund you

We will issue a full refund in these cases:

  • You were charged but did not receive your download — for example the app failed, crashed, or errored after payment and the file was never produced.
  • Duplicate payment — you were charged more than once for the same download.
  • Incorrect amount — you were charged an amount other than the ₹29 displayed.
  • Unauthorised payment — the payment was not authorised by you, subject to verification.

4. When we generally cannot refund

Because the product is digital and delivered instantly, we generally cannot refund once the file has been successfully delivered to you. In particular:

  • The download completed successfully and you simply changed your mind.
  • You are dissatisfied with the wording or style the AI produced. The AI output can be previewed for free before paying, and its quality is inherently subjective.
  • You did not get a job, interview, or any other outcome after using the resume. We make no guarantee of employment outcomes.
  • You entered incorrect information into your own resume.

If your situation isn't listed here, contact us anyway — genuine problems are reviewed case by case, and we would rather resolve it than leave you out of pocket.

5. How to request a refund

Email techieneelamsrivastava@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund request", within 7 days of the charge, including:

  • The payment / transaction ID (from your Razorpay receipt email, or your Google Play order confirmation).
  • The date and amount of the payment.
  • A short description of what went wrong.

7. Payments made through Google Play

If you paid using Google Play Billing, refunds may also be requested directly from Google, and are subject to Google Play's refund policy. Contact us as well and we will help.

8. Payments made through Razorpay

If you paid using Razorpay (UPI, card, or net banking), approved refunds are processed back through Razorpay to your original payment method.

9. Failed or pending payments

If money left your account but the payment shows as failed, it is usually an authorisation hold that is released automatically by your bank, typically within 5–7 business days. If it has not been released after that, contact us with your transaction ID and we will investigate.
